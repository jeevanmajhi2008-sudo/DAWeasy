## From idea to release in three steps

### Describe it

Type the song in your head, paste your lyrics, or pick a sound. The AI starts from your words and your Vision.

Click steps, lock the lyric lines you love, ask the producer for changes. Undo anything.

### Share and release it

Post to The Drop, keep every version, get feedback, then follow a dated release plan with cover ideas and captions.
